# CAN-005 · Licensing Canon

## 0. Scope and provenance

This canon governs the repositories of **Numen Games**, including Numinia's and
NWOS's. It does not govern the client *workspaces* generated by our tools
(§2.5).

Every section carries a provenance mark, so that future promotion to a canon of a
higher organization is mechanical and not a rewrite:

- **[UNIVERSAL]** — valid for any software project: SPDX, REUSE, the floor
  rule, the direction of pull, present≠distributed, the generator rule.
- **[NUMEN]** — specific to our business: the four regimes, VRM, CC0 catalog,
  reserved lore, trademark.

> This is not legal advice. It is the internal norm and its technical implementation.

---

## 1. The rule [NUMEN]

**Every piece is born in the most closed regime that makes sense and is opened by
deliberate acts, never by default.**

Four regimes, one per type of value:

| What | License | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Catalog — assets, data, metadata, design tokens | **CC0-1.0** | Given away: it is the acquisition channel, not the product |
| Edge — domain, UI, viewer, SDK, scripts, infrastructure | **MIT** | Shared: its value grows with adoption |
| Core — identity, progression, billing | **AGPL-3.0-only + CLA** | Free for those who share; paid for those who close |
| World and name — lore, narrative, brand | **Reserved** | The irreplicable is not licensed |

**Exception, recorded rather than hidden (ADR-036, 2026-09-01): the `canon/`
corpus itself is `CC0-1.0`.** The seven canon documents were first published on
2026-04-07 (`f765b99`) under the repository's root `CC0-1.0` `LICENSE`, four
months before the reservation regime existed. That grant is irrevocable, so the
reservation applied to `canon/**` on 2026-08-16 never took effect on them: it
described a state that was already legally impossible. `REUSE.toml` now declares
what is true instead of what was intended. The rule above still governs
everything born after the cut — including the lore in `numinia-lore`, which was
never under the open root license.

A file with no declared license is not neutral: the default is **all rights
reserved**. Declaring is what liberates.

**But silence does not declare.** Silence legally *amounts to* reservation; it does
not *express* it, and it is indistinguishable from an oversight. That is why the
reserved MUST be declared just like the open: with a `LICENSE` present whose text
expressly says that no right is granted, and with
`LicenseRef-Numen-AllRightsReserved` in `REUSE.toml`. Exactly where protecting
matters most is where one can least depend on the reader inferring correctly.

## 4. The irreversible [UNIVERSAL]

**Opening is an act. Closing is impossible for what is already published.** Granted
permissions are not revoked: a version published under MIT is MIT forever and
anyone can fork it from there.

| Position | Regime | Room to maneuver |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reserved, unpublished | Total |
| 2 | Copyleft **with** CLA | Total: can open further or license in parallel |
| 3 | Copyleft **without** CLA | Partial: requires each contributor's permission |
| 4 | Permissive, published | None in practice |
| 5 | CC0, published | None. The waiver is irrevocable |

**Birth license ≠ publication.** A repository is born with its `LICENSE` from the
first commit; while it is private it grants permission to no one.

**Two acts are irreversible and therefore signed by an Oracle**: writing to a
permanent store, and turning a repository from private to public. The second is
the one that surprises people — making the work publicly available under a licence
offer *is* the grant, with no `npm publish` and no Arweave needed.

**The checks each gate requires are in `STD-010`.** What is canon is that they are
acts and not operations.

## 7. The trademark [NUMEN]

CC0 does not surrender trademark rights. It is the only hard boundary.

Outside every free license remain the names **Numinia**, **Numen Games** and
**Khepri** as identifiers of origin, the logo, the isotype and the signature
wordmarks.

**Yes:** citing, linking, writing about the project, declaring that a product uses
Numinia assets. **No:** using them as one's own brand, suggesting sponsorship,
presenting a fork as official.

*The system can be copied; one cannot claim to be Numen.*

## Amendment — 2026-09-07

Sections 2, 3, 5, 6, 8 and 9 were emptied to pointers, and the two publication
gates in section 4 were moved. Their content is now `STD-010`, the licensing
standard, verbatim except for the corrections noted below.

**Why.** This canon carried 40 obligations — more than the other six canons
combined, which carry eleven between them. A canon states why; a standard states
what must be done. This document was doing both, and the second job was the
larger one.

**What this canon still decides.** The four regimes and why each exists (§1);
that silence does not declare (§1); that the `canon/` corpus is `CC0-1.0` by an
irrevocable grant (§1); that opening is an act and closing is impossible (§4);
that the trademark sits outside every free licence (§7).

**Section numbers were kept.** Every heading stays at its number and holds a
pointer, because 27 citations across the corpus name these sections by number —
including `REUSE.toml`, `TRADEMARKS.md`, `LICENSES/LicenseRef-Numen-AllRightsReserved.txt`
and `scripts/check-license-frontmatter.mjs`. Renumbering would break all of them
silently. The coupling is registered as `DBT-016`.

**Corrections made while moving, not before.** The `[UNIVERSAL]`/`[NUMEN]` marks
were dropped from the moved text: they record *scope for future promotion to a
higher organisation's canon*, which is a property of canon, not of a standard.
The conformance table in `STD-010` §7 states what this canon never did — that five
of its seven obligations have no guard, and that the AGPL-import lint rule §2
describes does not exist.

**Version.** Major, per `CORE-23`: obligations were withdrawn from this document.
They survive in `STD-010`, but a reader who complied with this canon alone would
now be under-informed.
