## Key Messages FAQ — Pablo FM, Oracle at Numinia

### What concepts do you want to be associated with?

Metaverse, Role Playing Games, Digital Goods, Data Sovereignty, Active Inference, AI, Mix Reality, Incentive System, Leveling Up, Art, Public Domain.

### Define yourself with 4 definitions.

- **15 Words:** Empower and transform organizational culture with engaging experiences that foster fun and drive impactful social change.
- **11 Words:** Foster organizational transformation and social change through engaging, fun experiences.
- **8 Words:** Cultivate fun, transformative experiences for organizational and social change.
- **7 Words:** Create fun, drive social change in organizations.

### What do you promote and what do you not allow?

We promote fun while building and making money, we do not allow toxic behavior during the game.

### Do you have a killer feature?

A game to work better

### Your 3 most important features?

Structured Functional Model, Organizational Operating System, Gamified Narrative Projection

### What benefits do they offer?

Build organizations to have better lives

### Who buys from you the most and who the least?

For now we are doing formations of what is web3 and the metaverse to companies.

### The ideal customer?

A gamer with an organization that loves the metaverse and the digital good and hates the old incentive systems.

### The highest aspiration of your customer?

Have better relations and a better life balance.

### What types of workers are you looking for?

Talented team, proactive builders that are willing to make a prosperous future for the ones that come behind.

### What excites those workers?

Being part of the future by doing something meaningful leaving a legacy, they build cathedrals not prefab homes.

### Present and future of your market?

We are just at the early stage the metaverse is just an experiment, for the super early adopter to experiment, in the future will impact the full digital landscape, like the email, like the P2P, like the web.

### What do your suppliers need?

Decentralize the systems and make them accessible.

### Who do you admire and who do you not?

I admire role game builders like the ones of the D&D. They changed popular culture for the better. I do not admire today's politicians.

### Degrees of transparency and awareness?

We build under public domain license CC0, we build in public blockchains, we try to have feedback loops to not be completely delusional.

### What legacy do you want to leave?

Built games for organizations with which to create a better world.

## Internal Aura Answers — Pablo FM, Oracle at Numinia

### Who are you and what do you do at Numinia?

Numinia represents the forefront of a meta-game evolution, a dreamscaped realm where knowledge and creativity merge. Our purpose is reshaping how organizations operate through the innovative paradigm of the game to work better.

### Why do you do it?

In a world that has rapidly outgrown traditional frameworks, we recognize the need for organizational evolution. Our core belief is that learning and working are enhanced through play, leading to a transformative workplace relationships.

### How do you do it/will do it?

Our path is one of deliberate, passionate progression, akin to crafting a timeless masterpiece. We blend innovation with heritage, driven by our core values and visionary purpose. Our approach: Remixing ideas to innovate.

### What do you believe in?

We believe in the liberating power of public domain information, and the interconnected nature of all creations. Our play is purposeful -- to leave behind a replicable, enduring, and useful model for future innovators.

### What sets you apart?

Numinia is not just a game; it's a transformative experience. Our Free to Play model fuses game mechanics with real-world impact, aiming to infuse fun and provoke social change in organizational settings.

### What do you lack and have in excess?

In our meta-game journey, the integration of social sciences is our current challenge. We are forging the first version of the game, a unique blend of play and cutting-edge technology, positioning ourselves in the expanding Metaverse.

### What is important vs. urgent?

The integrity and practicality of the game, aligned with its overarching purpose. The immediate goal is to develop a functional Proof of Concept for initial testing and refinement.

### In which phase are you?

In our nascent stage, 'The Repopulating of Numinia,' our mission is to lay down robust foundations for our city's growth, balancing meticulous planning with agile execution.

## External Aura Answers — Pablo FM, Oracle at Numinia

### Who do you want to reach?

We aim to connect with visionaries, innovators, and decision makers who challenge the status quo at organizations -- individuals and teams eager for a transformative digital experience.

### What does your competition do well and poorly?

We respect our competitors' strengths but observe their shortcomings in fully engaging and inspiring communities. Our unique blend of gaming and real-world application sets us apart.

### What do they praise and criticize about you?

We are lauded for our innovative approach and immersive experiences. Criticisms, mostly constructive, focus on the novelty of our concept. We welcome all feedback as opportunities for growth.

### Is their marketing true or greatly exaggerated?

While others may embellish their offerings, we prioritize authenticity. Our marketing is a true reflection of our capabilities and the experiences we provide.

### Is their culture true or greatly exaggerated?

There is often a gap between proclaimed culture and reality in our industry. We ensure our culture is genuinely practiced, focusing on team well-being and authenticity.

### How do you make yourself likable and retain customers and teams?

We cultivate a culture of innovation, respect, and continuous learning. Loyalty is built through transparent communication, exceptional experiences, and a commitment to the growth of our community and team.

### How do you protect yourself?

Our uniqueness in blending gaming, social change, and technology is our shield. We continually evolve to keep our offerings fresh, relevant, and less prone to imitation. Time and relevance are the key here.

### What legacy do you want to leave?

Our legacy extends beyond economic achievements. We aim to be remembered as pioneers who revolutionized organizational culture and relationships, leaving a lasting social impact.
