# MIS-086 — The real legal corpus enters numinia.com and the gate asks for its acceptance

> **Summary:** The real Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy (master in
> `numinia-nwos:operations/legal/`) replace numinia.com's placeholder
> drafts, and the `/lap/session/` login requires accepting the terms before
> connecting.
> **Epistemic:** How the legal corpus derived from the master reaches
> production without forking the source of truth.
> **Pragmatic:** numinia.com stops showing "DRAFT — pending Oracle review"
> on terms and privacy, and no session is created without recordable
> acceptance.
> **Audience:** numinia-web agent · Oracle

## Story

As an Oracle, I want numinia.com to publish the real legal documents and the
entry flow to require accepting them before login, so that no session exists
without an accepted legal framework.

---

## Verified premises (audit 2026-08-18, numinia-nwos agent)

The diagnosis is already done; the executor must re-audit whatever they
touch (AGT-01), but not start from zero:

1. numinia.com is served from `apps/store` (root wrangler.jsonc: worker
   `numinia-web`, routes numinia.com and www).
2. The legal pages ALREADY exist: `/legal/terms/`, `/legal/privacy/`,
   `/legal/cookies/`, `/legal/legal-notice/` ×5 locales, generated by
   `apps/store/src/pages/**/legal/[doc].astro` →
   `components/legal/LegalPage.astro` → inline content in
   `src/i18n/legal.ts` (drafts with `[PENDING]` placeholders and the
   "DRAFT — pending Oracle review" banner, MISSION-003 P3).
3. The footer (`components/chrome/SiteFooter.astro`) already links the four
   documents — no need to touch it.
4. The login is the `components/auth/LoginSpike.tsx` island (thirdweb +
   SIWE), mounted at `/lap/session/` via `SessionPage.astro` and at
   `/spike/auth`.
5. The masters live in `numinia-nwos:operations/legal/`
   (`O-004-terms-and-conditions-numengames.md` v1.0.0 EN,
   `O-003-privacy-policy-numengames.md` v1.1.0 ES). The master's FLAG-1
   note orders: what is published DERIVES from the master, never the
   reverse.
6. The docs glob (`components/docs/content.ts`) only covers
   `content/docs/**` — a new `content/legal/` does not collide.
7. REUSE: `apps/store/src/content/**` is annotated CC-BY-4.0; the legal
   texts are `LicenseRef-Numen-AllRightsReserved` and need their own,
   closer annotation (last-match-wins).

## Exception context (already recorded, do not re-litigate)

The Oracle ordered publishing WITH the review flags open (privacy
FLAG-2..6) and with the scope mismatch (the texts claim to govern
www.numen.games). Both are recorded in
`numinia-nwos:operations/O-002-contradictions.md` as **CON-004** and
**CON-005** — publishing is correct; resolving the flags belongs to another
session (the lawyer's / Oracle's). The texts are copied **verbatim**: no
content correction, not even the flagged typos.

## Execution log

**2026-08-18 — numinia-web agent (claude-opus-5), three reviewable cuts.**

Three decisions were raised to the Oracle before writing code, and all
three widened the written scope: (1) «recordable» means recorded in the
session, not just a UI gate; (2) the scope mismatch is declared in a note
outside the text; (3) the consent banner joins the mission.

**Cut 1 — the corpus (`720588c`).** Byte-for-byte copies of the two masters
in `apps/store/src/content/legal/{terms,privacy}.md` (sha256 verified
against `numinia-nwos:operations/legal/`). Registered via Vite glob in
`components/legal/content.ts` (the `components/docs/` pattern, because the
store does not use content collections). `LegalPage.astro` becomes a pure
dispatcher delegating to `LegalCorpusDoc.astro` (real) or
`LegalDraftDoc.astro` (cookies and legal notice stay in draft, with their
banner). The terms and privacy drafts were deleted from `i18n/legal.ts`:
the real text replaces them. `lib/legal.ts` pins the masters' versions and
a unit test fails if a refreshed copy strays from them. REUSE: a block for
`apps/store/src/content/legal/**` with `LicenseRef-Numen-AllRightsReserved`
AFTER the `content/**` block (CC-BY) — last-match-wins; `reuse lint`
confirms both files resolve to reserved.

**Cut 2 — the gate (`380186f`).** `LegalConsentGate.tsx` (checkbox + links
to both documents, with `data-metric`); the island does not mount
`ConnectEmbed` until it is ticked, and an already-authenticated session is
not asked. The real lock is the server: `POST /api/auth/login` responds 400
unless the body names `LEGAL_CORPUS_VERSION` (`terms@1.0.0+privacy@1.1.0`),
and the check runs BEFORE verifying the signature, so a login without
acceptance never reaches the provider. The accepted version travels inside
the signed session payload (`@numinia/auth`), optional in the schema so as
not to invalidate in-flight sessions. `SessionPanel` moved to its own file:
the island crossed 200 lines.

**Cut 3 — the notice (`bf0a139`).** The Layer 0 banner left the lorem
ipsum: real copy in five languages naming exactly what the platform stores
(one cookie for the notice, another for the session, preferences and
profile in the browser, no third-party tracking), a link added to
`/legal/privacy/` and `CONSENT_VERSION` to `2026-08-18` — every acceptance
of the placebo text is asked again. The acceptance suite fails if «lorem
ipsum» reappears in a build.

**Release `v0.46.0` (`1243a07`)** with its card on the timeline.

**Live (2026-08-18 11:36 UTC, SHA `1243a07`).** Checked against
numinia.com: `/legal/terms/` (v1.0.0, no draft banner) and
`/legal/privacy/` (v1.1.0) with the scope note in all five locales and the
language notice in all but its own; `/legal/cookies/` keeps its draft; the
banner serves `data-consent-version="2026-08-18"` with no trace of lorem
ipsum; the `/es/lap/session/` gate arrives with the checkbox already in the
served HTML; and `POST /api/auth/login` without acceptance — or with an
expired one — responds
`400 {"error":"Legal acceptance is required","required":"terms@1.0.0+privacy@1.1.0"}`.

Verification: `npm run ci` green (28 tasks), 28 Gherkin scenarios, 180

## Execution Reality

*(Fill when closing the mission — the real plans vs the ideal plans)*

- **Technology/approach used:** as planned (verbatim copies + Vite glob +
  gate in the island), plus two things the brief did not ask for: the
  acceptance recorded inside the signed session and the real cookie banner.
- **Why it diverged:** the AC said «no session is created without
  recordable acceptance» while the bullet only described a UI gate. Asked,
  the Oracle chose to genuinely record — which forced touching
  `@numinia/auth`. The banner joined because publishing the real corpus
  behind a Latin placeholder notice was a visible incoherence on the same
  screen.
- **Key learning:** the CSS betrays the dispatcher. A component that only
  renders one branch still ships the other branch's styles in the route's
  bundle, so an `html.includes('data-legal-draft')` gave a false positive
  on an already-published page. Markup assertions must look at the element
  (`/<p[^>]*data-…/`), not the HTML's plain text. Second finding: the
  island renders on the server and hydrates afterwards — ticking the
  checkbox before hydration is a click React discards, and the e2e has to
  wait for `networkidle`.
- **Closing date:** 2026-08-18
- **Executing agent:** claude-opus-5 (numinia-web session)
